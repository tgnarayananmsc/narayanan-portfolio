🚘 ANPR-Based Smart Toll Payment & Vehicle Management System

M.Sc. Electronics · 2025ANPRVehicle Management

Overview

A smart toll workflow that uses Automatic Number Plate Recognition to identify vehicles and support payment and vehicle management without stopping to hand over cash or tickets.

Objectives

  • Detect and read vehicle number plates automatically
  • Support smart toll payment workflows
  • Maintain vehicle records for management

Key features

  • Automatic number plate recognition
  • Toll payment and vehicle management workflow
  • Faster, contactless toll handling
  • Vehicle record keeping

Technology & components

Camera / image captureANPR processingEmbedded hardwareSoftware & database

Outcome

A complete prototype presented as my postgraduate final-year project, combining hardware, image processing and software.
