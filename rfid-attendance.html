UG FINAL-YEAR PROJECT

📡 RFID-Based Real-Time Attendance System

B.Sc. Electronics · 2023RFIDEmbedded

Overview

An attendance system that identifies each person through an RFID tag and records attendance automatically in real time, replacing manual roll calls and reducing errors and proxy attendance.

Objectives

  • Automate attendance marking using RFID identification
  • Record entries with date and time without manual effort
  • Make attendance records quick to review

Key features

  • Contactless tag-based identification
  • Real-time attendance recording
  • Fast, accurate and difficult to misuse
  • Simple for students and staff to use

Technology & components

RFID reader & tagsMicrocontrollerDisplay / indicatorsEmbedded programmingData logging

Outcome

A working prototype that demonstrates reliable, real-time attendance capture and formed the base of my undergraduate final-year project.
